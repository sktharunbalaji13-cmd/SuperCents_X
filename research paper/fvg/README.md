# FVG — Fair Value Gap (Imbalance)

## What it is (retail / Smart Money Concepts terminology)
A **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** is a 3-candle price imbalance: the *low of candle 1* is above the *high of candle 3* (bullish FVG) — i.e. a gap left between candle 1 and candle 3 when candle 2 moves aggressively. Also called an **imbalance** or broken-wing butterfly. SMC treats the empty zone as "unfair price" that price tends to **return to (fill/mitigate)** before continuing. FVGs are used as retracement/entry zones, similar to order blocks, but are defined entirely by the 3-bar structure.

## Academic analog
The academic counterpart is research on **price gaps and market-depth/imbalance** structures — empty price levels and their predictive content for future moves.

## Papers in this folder
1. **1405.1247 — Stylized facts of price gaps in limit order books: Evidence from Chinese stocks (Gu, Xiong, Zhang, Chen, Zhang, Zhou)**
   Documents empirical properties of price gaps (power-law tails, long-range correlation, buy/sell asymmetry) in order books — a data-level characterisation of the "gap/imbalance" phenomenon FVGs exploit.
2. **1201.5448 — Determinants of immediate price impacts at the trade level in an emerging order-driven market**
   Studies the drivers of immediate price moves (order size, book liquidity, gaps) — the micro-mechanics behind why an unfilled gap zone tends to pull price back toward it.

## Additional papers (batch 2)
3. **2605.11423 — A Validated Volatility-Volume-Gap Classifier for Regime Identification in MNQ Intraday Data (M. Mesfin)**
   Classifies days by overnight gap + first-30-min return + first-bar volume and studies intraday gap-following/reversal regimes — an empirical take on gap-based (FVG) behaviour and its tradability.
