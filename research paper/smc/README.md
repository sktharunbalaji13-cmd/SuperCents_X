# SMC — Smart Money Concepts (Institutional Flow)

## What it is (retail taxonomy umbrella)
**Smart Money Concepts (SMC)** is the umbrella retail framework built by ICT and popularised via TradingView tools (~2020s). It argues markets are moved by large "smart money" institutions, and that their footprints appear as visible **market structure** — swings, BOS, CHoCH/MSS, order blocks, FVGs and liquidity sweeps. SMC is a *vocabulary* over these structures rather than a distinct scientific model. The academic foundations lie in **market microstructure** and **informed-trading theory** (how informed/institutional order flow moves prices and reveals itself).

## Academic analog
The rigorous analogue of "smart money" is the **informed / institutional trader** studied in microstructure (adverse selection, Kyle's model, Glosten–Milgrom, order splitting and herding). These papers describe how large, informed flow is structured, detected and how it impacts price.

## Papers in this folder
1. **1108.1632 — Why is order flow so persistent? (Lillo, Mikhail, Farmer, ...)**
   Distinguishes **herding** from **order splitting** by large institutional traders using London Stock Exchange data — the empirical fingerprint of "smart money" breaking large orders into many smaller ones (the behaviour SMC tries to detect as engineered footprints).
2. **2608.04373 — Public Trader Identity: Adverse Selection and Return Predictability**
   Studies how identifiable, informed ("smart") traders are adversely selected against and leave return-predictable footprints — a modern, data-driven analogue of the smart-money informational edge.

## Additional papers (batch 2)
3. **1610.00261 — Limit Order Strategic Placement with Adverse Selection Risk and the Role of Latency (Lehalle, Mounjid)**
   Empirically and theoretically shows how informed (smart) flow is resisted via limit orders, adverse-selection exposure, and why speed/latency protects market makers — the mechanics of smart-money interaction.
4. **2107.12094 — Liquidity Provision with Adverse Selection and Inventory Costs (Baruch & Glosten-class dealer model)**
   Models dealers competing for client order flow without knowing whether the client trades on information — formalises the adverse-selection cost of trading against smart money.
